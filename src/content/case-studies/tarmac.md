problem: "HGV drivers at Tarmac's Aberthaw facility were forgetting to raise the loading steps before pulling away, and the equipment was being damaged as a result. Several on-site incidents made it clear the existing reminders weren't landing at the moment that mattered: as the driver got back into the cab."
installed: "A trial AudioSign, triggered by movement, despatched to site the same week as the first conversation. It speaks to the driver at the point of departure: a reminder to raise the steps, delivered every time, whether or not anyone is looking at the sign. After the trial, further signs were installed across six loading bays."
changed: "Tarmac recorded zero incidents across the six bays over the three months that followed, 1 September to 30 November 2023. The site wrote the installation up as a Health and Safety Good Practice note and circulated it across the business, with AudioSigns named as the supplier."
